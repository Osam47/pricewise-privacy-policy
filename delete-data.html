Data Deletion Request — PriceWise

Developer: Octadrop LLC  |  App: PriceWise (com.octadrop.pricewise)

If you would like to request the deletion of your data associated with PriceWise, please follow the steps below. We will process your request within 30 days.

How to request data deletion

  1. Send an email to pricewise@octadrop.com with the subject line:
    Data Deletion Request - PriceWise
  2. In the body of your email, include:
    • The mention "PriceWise data deletion"
    • The approximate date you first used the app (if known)
    • Any other details that may help us identify your data
  3. We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Send deletion request

What data is deleted

The table below shows what happens to each type of data when a deletion request is processed:

Data type Description Action Retention after request
User account (UID) Anonymous Firebase identifier linked to your activity Deleted Within 30 days
Interaction history Your scan history, searches, and price updates linked to your UID Deleted Within 30 days
404 scans Barcodes you scanned that returned no result Deleted Within 30 days
Community product data Product names, brands, and prices you submitted — shared with all users Kept (anonymised) Retained indefinitely to preserve community database integrity. Your UID is removed from these records.
Error reports Product corrections you submitted Kept (anonymised) Your UID is removed; the report content may be retained for data quality purposes.

Note on anonymous authentication

PriceWise uses anonymous authentication — no email address or password is required to use the app. Your identity is represented solely by a randomly generated ID (UID) stored on your device. If you uninstall the app, this UID is lost and you will no longer have access to your personal activity data. You may still submit a deletion request to ensure any remaining server-side data linked to your former UID is removed.
